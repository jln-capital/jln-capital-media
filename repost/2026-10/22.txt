Notes No. 1

A trading account that loses 50% needs a 100% gain just to get back to where it started. A loss and the gain that repairs it are never the same size.

After a drawdown, the next gain is earned on a smaller balance. An account that falls from 100 to 50 has to double to return to 100. The gain needed to recover a loss d is d / (1 − d): 11.1% after a 10% loss, 25% after a 20% loss, 100% after a 50% loss. This is why the depth of a drawdown matters more than it first appears.
