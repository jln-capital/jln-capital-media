Is a trading system that wins 90% of its trades always profitable?

No. What a system earns per trade is its expectancy: the win rate times the average win, minus the loss rate times the average loss. A system that wins 90% of the time with an average gain of 1 and an average loss of 10 earns 0.9 × 1 − 0.1 × 10 = −0.1 per trade. Over 100 trades that is 90 wins worth +90 and 10 losses worth −100, a net result of −10. A win rate says how often a system is right, not how much it makes.
